## Paper 5: A direct neural signature of serial dependence in working memory

**Fischer, C., Kaiser, J. & Bledowski, C. — eLife (2025/2026)**
DOI: 10.7554/eLife.99478.2 | Reviewed preprint 2025-2026

**Abstract:** MEG during working memory task with sequential motion directions. Multivariate analysis reconstructed motion directions from brain signals. Crucially, reconstructed directions were attractively shifted towards the previous trial's target. Neural bias emerged at later, post-encoding time points — serial dependence affects memorized information during read-out and reactivation, not early perceptual stages.
